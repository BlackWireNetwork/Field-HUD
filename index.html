FIELD MANUAL & WORKFLOW GUIDE

Complete breakdown of app buttons, features, and professional field use cases.

1. Top HUD Toolbar Buttons

MENU: Opens project files, data exports, CAD layers, and settings.
BAT (Battery Saver): Turns screen black to save battery while keeping GPS tracking alive.
SET: Access coordinate systems, units, and tolerances.
GPS: Instantly re-centers map on your current location.
HLP: Opens this field manual.

2. Point Storing & QC Averaging

STORE GPS: Triggers a 5-second multi-epoch averaging countdown to cancel out phone GPS jitter.
STORE MAP: Instantly drops a point right where the red crosshairs are centered.
SNAP PHOTO: Attaches a camera image inspection to your next stored point.

3. Smart Linework & Feature Codes

Type codes like EP (Edge of Pavement) or BOC (Back of Curb) in the search bar. Use modifier buttons: B starts a new line string, and E terminates it automatically on the map.

4. COGO Suite & Stakeout Navigation

INVERSE: Tap two map points to compute bearing and distance.
STAKE OUT: Locks onto a point to view real-time distance, delta North/East, and a visual bullseye radar screen.

RTK GNSS CONNECTIVITY GUIDE

Choose your device platform below to connect an external Bluetooth receiver for true RTK accuracy:

Android Users: NTRIP Connection

1. Pair your Bluetooth GNSS receiver in your Android Bluetooth settings.
2. Download a free client app like Lefebure NTRIP Client or SW Maps from the Play Store.
3. Input your state VRS network credentials (Caster IP, Port, Mountpoint, Username, Password).
4. Connect. The app pulls cellular corrections and pipes them to your receiver for live survey precision.

iPhone / iOS Users: NTRIP Connection

1. Pair your Bluetooth GNSS receiver to your iOS device.
2. Use a compatible free iOS app with built-in Bluetooth and NTRIP support, such as SW Maps or NTRIP STX (or your receiver brand's official app like Emlid Flow).
3. Enter your local RTK Network / VRS caster parameters.
4. Start the correction stream to instantly feed high-precision coordinates into your browser session.
